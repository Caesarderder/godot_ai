# Playtest session protocol

## Before the session

- Record build identifier, commit or artifact hash, Godot version, browser/device, viewport, input method, locale, and save state.
- Select one primary hypothesis and a short observation window.
- Prepare a clean start state and a way to capture notes without interrupting play.
- Decide what ordinary launch instructions a real player would see; do not add verbal coaching.

## During the session

Timestamp observable events. Prefer “looked at the pause screen for 18 seconds and selected Resume twice” over “menu was confusing.” Note interventions separately because they change the validity of later observations.

Capture at minimum:

- first meaningful input;
- first understood goal;
- first success and first failure;
- retries or voluntary continuation;
- visible confusion, workaround, or abandonment;
- browser, input, audio, save, or performance faults.

## After the session

Ask a few neutral questions:

1. What did you think you were trying to do?
2. What made you choose that action?
3. What was confusing or surprising?
4. What would you try next?

Do not reveal the intended answer before these questions. Thank the participant; do not debate their experience.

## Privacy

Collect only data required by the hypothesis. Obtain consent for recordings or telemetry, avoid unnecessary personal data, and follow the project's retention/deletion policy.
