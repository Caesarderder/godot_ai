# Narrative Contract Reference

## Beat contract

| Beat | Entry state | Player action/choice | Response | Consequence | State owner | Reset/persistence | Next playable state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

A beat is incomplete when its trigger, state owner, or next playable state is unknown.

## Branch contract

- availability condition and how it is communicated;
- player-understandable stakes;
- immediate response and downstream consequence;
- flag/value owner, save behavior, and migration/default state;
- convergence point or explicit permanent divergence;
- fallback when content, actor, or prerequisite is unavailable.

## Character contract

- observable goal and immediate tactic;
- pressure or opportunity created for the player;
- gameplay function and relationship state;
- information the character knows, hides, or misunderstands;
- boundary or contradiction that prevents generic behavior;
- change demonstrated through action rather than description.

## Environmental storytelling

Use placement, traversal, damage/change, props, sound, and repeated motifs to reveal cause and consequence. Verify the clue is visible from the likely approach and remains coherent after gameplay state changes. Required objectives and rules need redundant delivery.

## Localization readiness

- stable key and speaker/scene context;
- variables named by meaning rather than position;
- no sentence assembly that assumes one grammar;
- plural, gender, formality, timing, and space notes where relevant;
- translated text never controls a branch condition;
- truncation and fallback behavior identified for UI or timed delivery.

## Evidence levels

- **Authored:** content and state contract exist.
- **Integrated:** current runtime data/scenes/scripts connect the contract.
- **Path exercised:** trigger, response, consequence, reset, and persistence ran.
- **Player observed:** an uncoached player produced relevant comprehension or emotional evidence.

Do not claim a narrative outcome from authored or integrated content alone.
