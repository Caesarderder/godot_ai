# Original MOBA production contract

`project-c` will be an original competitive MOBA, not a reproduction of another game's protected expression.

## Target match

- Two teams of five select from original heroes, contest top/mid/bottom lanes, destroy towers and finally the opposing relay core.
- A short local-bot match is the first playable production milestone; authoritative online networking, matchmaking, anti-cheat, live operations, ranked balance, accessibility research, voice and a content-production pipeline are separate, required release tracks.
- Current implemented facts are a local three-lane match kernel, five selectable original heroes with distinct authoritative Q outcomes, towers/cores, minion pressure, shop growth, hero death/respawn and deterministic victory, plus the preserved single-lane slice. This is not proof of a balanced full match or release-ready MOBA.

## Gate sequence

1. Three-lane local match: team spawning, minions, towers, core and deterministic victory.
2. Five original hero kits, item shop, level/skill progression and bot decision loops.
3. Local full-match playtest and repeatable functional/performance evidence.
4. Network authority/reconciliation, then security, scale and player research gates.

No step may use copied characters, names, art, maps, sound, UI, code, or match data from commercial games.
